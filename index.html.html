THE MATERIAL ADVANTAGE

A stronger profile.
A smoother journey.

Profiled for structural performance

The corrugated outer wall provides ring stiffness to help resist external loads. Selection depends on soil conditions, installation and project design.

Smooth on the inside

The smooth inner wall supports hydraulic flow and limits the build-up of deposits in correctly designed systems.

Practical to handle and connect

Polyethylene combines relatively low weight with corrosion resistance. Corrugated systems use couplings and sealing rings for connection.
